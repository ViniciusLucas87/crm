# SEO Authority Playbook — Pacific North Systems

**Status**: Active | **Last updated**: 2026-08-03 | **Owner**: Marketing

## Objective

Build legitimate, earned authority for pacificnorthsystems.com in Canadian SMB automation and custom software search results. Zero paid links, zero link exchanges, zero automated outreach. Every link target is evaluated for relevance, audience, and editorial standards.

## Target Audience

Canadian small and mid-size business owners and operators (5-200 employees), primarily in British Columbia and Ontario, who are investigating automation, custom software, CRM, or workflow improvement.

## Earned-Link Target Categories

### 1. Canadian Chambers of Commerce and Boards of Trade
- **Why**: Authoritative .org domains, local relevance, member resource pages
- **Approach**: Offer a free guest article on "automation for SMBs" or a free tool listing in member resources
- **Examples**: Vancouver Board of Trade, Surrey Board of Trade, Burnaby Board of Trade, BC Chamber of Commerce, Canadian Chamber of Commerce
- **Message angle**: "We've published free tools and research that your members may find useful for operational planning"

### 2. CFIB-Style Small Business Communities
- **Why**: Direct SMB owner audience, high trust
- **Approach**: Contribute to member forums or resource libraries with genuinely useful content
- **Examples**: Canadian Federation of Independent Business, Small Business BC, local business improvement associations
- **Message angle**: "Free operational planning tools — no signup, no sales pitch"

### 3. Provincial Business Associations
- **Why**: Authority domains, regional relevance
- **Approach**: Offer industry-specific content or tools
- **Examples**: BC Tech Association, Ontario Business Improvement Area Associations, Manufactured in BC
- **Message angle**: "Free calculators for estimating automation ROI — built with Canadian data"

### 4. Accountants and Bookkeepers
- **Why**: Trusted advisors to SMB owners; frequently asked about automation and software
- **Approach**: Provide a resource page they can share with clients
- **Message angle**: "Free tools your clients can use to estimate manual work costs and automation ROI — no signup required"

### 5. Operations Consultants
- **Why**: Directly relevant audience; potential referral partners
- **Approach**: Share research and tools as resources for their client work
- **Message angle**: "Citation-backed Canadian SMB operations benchmarks — free to use and share"

### 6. Local Vancouver/BC Publications
- **Why**: Regional relevance, local business audience
- **Approach**: Pitch contributed articles on Canadian SMB automation trends
- **Examples**: Business in Vancouver, BC Business, Vancouver Sun business section, Daily Hive Vancouver
- **Message angle**: "How Vancouver SMBs are using automation to address labour challenges — with free planning tools"

### 7. Colleges and Incubators
- **Why**: Educational authority, entrepreneurship programs
- **Approach**: Offer tools as classroom resources for business/entrepreneurship students
- **Examples**: BCIT, UBC Sauder, SFU Beedie, Douglas College, entrepreneurship@UBC, local incubators
- **Message angle**: "Free business planning calculators for entrepreneurship students"

### 8. Directories and Resource Lists
- **Why**: Citation authority, referral traffic
- **Approach**: Submit to relevant, editorially-reviewed directories only
- **Examples**: Canadian business software directories, SMB resource lists, BC business directories
- **Message angle**: Factual listing with accurate description

### 9. Podcasts and Webinars
- **Why**: Deep engagement, backlinks from show notes
- **Approach**: Pitch as a guest on Canadian business, tech, and entrepreneurship podcasts
- **Topics**: "What Canadian SMBs should automate first," "The real cost of manual paperwork," "Build vs. buy software for SMBs"
- **Message angle**: "Practical, no-hype automation advice for Canadian business owners"

## Outreach Qualification Checklist

Before contacting any target, verify:
- [ ] The site is relevant to Canadian SMBs, automation, custom software, or business operations
- [ ] The site has genuine editorial standards (not a link farm or paid directory)
- [ ] The audience matches our target (SMB owners/operators, not enterprise or consumers)
- [ ] Our content or tools would genuinely benefit their audience
- [ ] We are not asking for a link — we are offering useful content
- [ ] The contact method is appropriate (not spam, not purchased lists)
- [ ] We have personalized the outreach with specific reference to their audience/content

## Prohibited Practices

- No purchased links or paid placements
- No fake reviews or testimonials
- No link exchanges ("I'll link to you if you link to me")
- No automated or bulk unsolicited email
- No comment spam or forum signature links
- No private blog network (PBN) links
- No cloaking, redirects, or doorway pages
- No keyword-stuffed anchor text in outreach

## Tracking Fields

For each outreach target:
- Organization name, URL, contact person, contact method
- Date contacted, follow-up date, response received
- Outcome: no response, declined, link placed (URL), guest post published (URL), podcast recorded (URL)
- Notes: personalization details, relationship status
